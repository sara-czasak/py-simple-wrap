    How to get your name here? 🛠️

    I welcome contributions of all sizes! Whether you are fixing a typo in the README, suggesting a new "Simple" module, reporting a bug, or writing code, I'd love to have you involved — and to give you a tile of your very own.

    Check out CONTRIBUTING.md to see how you can get started!

    🗺️ And which team will you join?

    The Module Magicians. The Code Conjurers. The Test Sentinels. The Lore Keepers. The Bugbear Slayers. The Dwarven Engineers. Every guild needs more hands — pick the one that calls to you and open a PR.

    ⭐ Star the repo if py-simple-wrap made your life easier — it costs nothing and it's how the next beginner finds us.

    🤝 Read CONTRIBUTING.md and claim your tile.
